## 1. Metric definitions in our own words

| Metric | What it tells an executive | How we calculate it | When it is "not available" |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adoption** | What share of the people who have a licence actually used the tool last week. | Weekly active users in the latest supplied week ÷ licensed users. | No licence count, or no usage reported for the latest week. |
| **Weekly active users – change** | Whether usage is growing or shrinking across the three supplied weeks. | (Latest-week WAU − first-week WAU) ÷ first-week WAU. A missing middle week does not matter. | First or latest week missing. |
| **Return on investment (ROI)** | How much value came back for every dollar spent so far. +20% means USD 1.20 of benefit per USD 1 of cost. | (Realised benefit to date − total cost to date) ÷ total cost to date. | No cost or no benefit data. |
| **Average monthly benefit** | The typical benefit delivered in a month since go-live. | Realised benefit to date ÷ whole months live. | Go-live date unknown. |
| **Payback period (months)** | How many months of today's average benefit it takes to cover everything spent so far. | Total cost to date ÷ average monthly benefit. | Average monthly benefit is zero or negative ("payback not reached"). |
| **Run-rate versus plan** | Whether the current pace of benefit, annualised, meets the benefit promised in the plan. 100% = on plan. | Average monthly benefit × 12 ÷ planned annual benefit. | Planned annual benefit not supplied. |
| **Portfolio status** | One traffic light per initiative. | **Green**: adoption ≥ 60% **and** ROI > 0. **Red**: adoption < 30% **or** ROI < 0. **Amber**: everything else. | Never: an initiative with missing adoption or ROI is Amber and the reason says so. |

All monetary values are in USD. Percentages are shown to one decimal place; the stored values keep full precision.

## 2. Assumptions

| # | Assumption | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | *Months live* = whole calendar months from go-live to the reporting date; a partial month does not count. | Matches the monthly cost and benefit rows (one row per completed month). |
| A2 | The reporting date is the last day (Sunday) of the latest supplied week. Cost and benefit "to date" include every monthly row up to that date. | The usage feed defines the reporting period; finance feeds are monthly. |
| A3 | Dates that are not ISO are read day-first (DD/MM/YYYY). Usage dates must land on one of the supplied reporting weeks, which confirms the reading. | The source systems use a UK locale; the week check rules out month-first readings. |
| A4 | *Weekly active users* is the one canonical usage measure. `WAU`, `weekly_active_users` and `Active Users (7d)` are the same measure; monthly active users (`MAU`) is a different measure and is excluded. | Different teams name the same measure differently; MAU would overstate weekly adoption. |
| A5 | Weekly active users cannot exceed licensed users. A value that does is corrected only when removing one extra zero gives a value within licences and within 25% of the neighbouring week; otherwise it is left missing. | Prevents typos from producing adoption above 100% without inventing data. |
| A6 | A negative cost with no credit-note category is a sign error and is made positive. | There is no refund/credit category in the cost feed. |
| A7 | Amounts in kUSD are multiplied by 1,000. Benefits in other currencies are converted with the Group Finance planning rate in `fx_rates.csv` (EUR 1 = USD 1.08). | One currency and unit for every comparison. |
| A8 | Portfolio totals (total cost, total benefit, portfolio ROI = total benefit vs total cost, portfolio adoption = total latest-week WAU ÷ total licences) are team-defined aggregates, labelled as such. | Executives ask for a portfolio view; the brief defines metrics per initiative only. |
| A9 | Missing values are never imputed. A metric that depends on a missing value is shown as "not available" with the reason. | No invented numbers (FR-03, FR-08). |
| A10 | Boundaries: adoption of exactly 60% can be Green; exactly 30% is not Red; ROI of exactly 0 is Amber (Green needs ROI above 0, Red needs ROI below 0). | Literal reading of the status rule. |
| A11 | The data files are the system of record for every number. Figures inside charters (for example a charter's "expected annual benefit") are narrative context and are never used in calculations. | One governed source per number; charters can be out of date. |
| A12 | Approved personal data = executive sponsor and product owner names and job titles, as listed in the register and charters. Any other personal detail is refused. | FR-08 guardrail. |

## 3. Clarification questions for the client

1. **(Material) Which planned annual benefit is authoritative?** The register and the charters disagree for at least one initiative (INIT-01: register USD 300,000, charter "expected annual benefit of USD 350,000"). We use the register (A11). If the charter is authoritative, INIT-01's run-rate versus plan falls from 96.0% to about 82%, and the weekly report would flag it as behind plan.
2. Is realised benefit meant to be cash received or value accrued? Two feeds use different reporting bases (monthly vs cumulative-to-date); we converted cumulative values to monthly increments (see the change log).
3. Should ROI include only costs since go-live, or also pre-go-live build costs? The cost feed starts in each initiative's go-live month.
