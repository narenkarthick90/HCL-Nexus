Invoice Document AI: Initiative Charter

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-02-01 · Owner: Sofia Marquez

Initiative overview

FieldValue
Initiative IDINIT-04
Initiative nameInvoice Document AI
TypeDocument AI
Business unitFinance
Go-live date2026-02-01

Extract invoice fields automatically and route exceptions to accounts-payable clerks, so that invoices are posted faster and with fewer manual keying errors.

Scope

In scope

  • Supplier invoices received by email and portal
  • Exception queue for the 120 accounts-payable users
  • Posting to the ERP after clerk validation

Out of scope

  • Expense receipts
  • Supplier onboarding

Benefit hypothesis

Benefit comes from clerk hours saved per invoice and from early-payment discounts captured because invoices are processed sooner.

KPIs and measurement

  • Straight-through processing rate
  • Clerk weekly active usage of the exception queue
  • Early-payment discounts captured

Risks and mitigations
