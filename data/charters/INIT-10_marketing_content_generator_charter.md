# Marketing Content Generator: Initiative Charter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Initiative name | Marketing Content Generator |
| Type | Generative AI |
| Business unit | Marketing |

Draft first versions of campaign copy, product descriptions and social posts in the Nimbus brand voice for review by the content team.

## Scope

### In scope

- Campaign and product copy in five languages
- Brand-voice guardrails
- The 300 licensed marketing users

### Out of scope

- Publishing without human review
- Paid media buying

## Benefit hypothesis

Benefit is agency spend avoided for first drafts and translation.
