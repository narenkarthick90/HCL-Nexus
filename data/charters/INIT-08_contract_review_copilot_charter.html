Contract Review Copilot: Initiative Charter

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-07-01 · Owner: Kwame Asante

Initiative overview

FieldValue
Initiative IDINIT-08
Initiative nameContract Review Copilot
TypeCopilot
Business unitLegal
Go-live date2026-07-01

Help lawyers and contract managers review third-party contracts faster by flagging deviations from the Nimbus clause playbook.

Scope

In scope

  • Supplier and customer contracts in English
  • Clause deviation flags with playbook references
  • The 250 licensed legal and procurement users

Out of scope

  • Signing or approving contracts
  • Litigation documents

Benefit hypothesis

Benefit is measured as external counsel spend avoided and lawyer hours saved per contract review.

KPIs and measurement

  • Review cycle time
  • Weekly active users
  • External counsel spend

Risks and mitigations
